## 💰 Personal Loan Campaign Analysis (Machine Learning Project)

### 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Machine Learning course** in the **PGP-AIML-Online program at Great Learning**.

The objective is to help **AllLife Bank** improve its marketing strategy by identifying customers who are most likely to purchase personal loans.

---

### 🎯 Objectives

* Predict the likelihood of customers purchasing personal loans
* Identify key factors influencing loan acceptance
* Segment customers for targeted marketing campaigns
* Improve conversion rates and revenue growth

---

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Python
* NumPy
* Pandas
* Matplotlib & Seaborn
* Scikit-learn

---

### 📊 Project Workflow

#### 🔹 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

* Customer demographics analysis
* Income, credit card usage, and account behavior
* Correlation between features

#### 🔹 Feature Engineering

* Selecting important variables influencing loan decisions

#### 🔹 Model Building

* Logistic Regression
* Decision Tree Classifier

#### 🔹 Clustering

* Customer segmentation using clustering techniques

---

### 📈 Key Insights

* Income and account balance significantly influence loan acceptance
* Customers with higher credit card usage show higher probability
* Certain customer segments are more responsive to loan campaigns

---

### 💡 Business Recommendations

* Target high-income and high-engagement customers
* Personalize marketing campaigns based on customer segments
* Focus on customers with strong financial activity
* Use predictive models for efficient campaign targeting
